# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the ssher community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Protect other people’s privacy and security.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer’s GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential as far as reasonably possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
